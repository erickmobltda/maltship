# Changelog

## 2026-11-03 — Real catalog

**Why:** replace the 20 samples with a real catalog built from public datasets.

- Catalog: about 2,200 real whiskies with Whisky Advocate scores (filter by score), a flavour profile from *Whisky Classified* for Scotch single malts, and the region of each Scotch distillery. The 20 sample items keep their ids.
- Bottle photos from Open Food Facts where it has them, credited on the item page.

## 2026-10-30 — First version

**Why:** the first whisky app built from the [collector-app-blueprint](https://github.com/erickmobltda/collector-app-blueprint), to try the blueprint end to end.

- Discover, item page, shelf (quantity, sealed/open), wishlist, ratings with likes, profiles, follows, Following/Everyone feeds, find people, settings, 18+ gate, demo account.
- EN and PT-BR, chosen by the user.
- Catalog: 20 sample whiskies (real brands, illustrative specs) in `data/sample.json`.
